# Code of conduct

CHC is used and built by people from many countries, in many languages, with every level of computer
knowledge. Everyone is welcome here.

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new to computers or to GitHub.
- Write in English when you can, but no one is turned away for writing in their own language.
- Criticize ideas and code, not people.
- No harassment, insults, discrimination or sharing other people's private information.
- Don't post spam or advertising.

Comments, issues and discussions that break these rules can be edited, hidden or removed, and repeated
breaks can lead to a ban from the project. To report a problem, contact the maintainer through
[GitHub](https://github.com/aliinal0) or use GitHub's "Report content" option.
